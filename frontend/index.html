    About SignBridge

    SignBridge turns English, spoken, typed or from a PDF, into a sequence of recorded Auslan signs played on a 2D figure. The motion comes from about 3,300 recorded signs (a dictionary corpus and Auslan Signbank), tracked with MediaPipe.

    It is not an Auslan translator and does not replace a qualified Auslan interpreter. Auslan is a language of its own, with its own grammar; it is not English on the hands.

    • Signs are chosen automatically, word by word, with a few simple ordering rules. Auslan grammar (use of space, facial grammar, classifiers) is not modelled.
    • Words without a usable recorded sign are fingerspelled. Fingerspelling is a fallback, not the natural sign.
    • “Needs review” marks a best guess among several meanings, or motion tracking flagged by automated checks.
    • Nothing here has been verified by a fluent Auslan signer or Deaf community members.
    • Sources: about 3,200 signs from a recorded Auslan dictionary corpus, and 89 from Auslan Signbank (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, non-commercial; each sign's Signbank page is kept for attribution). Fingerspelling letters A–Z partly from Signbank.
    • Voice input uses your browser's own speech recognition. SignBridge does not record, store or upload audio, but Chrome and Edge send it to their speech service to turn it into text.

    How it works

    • Press Speak (or M) and say a short English sentence. When you stop, the words appear as text and the signs play. Press again or Esc to stop early. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.
    • Or type text and press Translate (or Enter; Shift+Enter adds a line).
    • The figure plays each sign in turn. Below it you can see the sign being shown, and the matching English words.
    • The sign sequence lists every sign: ✓ available sign, ABC fingerspelled because no recorded sign is available, ⚠ needs review, ✕ unavailable. Select one to jump to it.
    • Upload PDF to get the document's key points; select a point to sign it.
    • Keys: M speak, Space play/pause, ← → previous/next sign, Home restart, F full screen.
    • Translation details shows how each word was handled and lets you change it. Advanced settings has the source recording, the gloss line and playback options.
